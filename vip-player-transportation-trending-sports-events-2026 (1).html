VIP & Player Transportation for 2026's Biggest Events

Super Bowl LXI, Ryder Cup 2027, Garcia vs Benn, Money in the Bank, LA28 Olympics — chauffeur, airport transfer and venue transport guide for the events driving the most search demand right now.

August 6, 2026 NoirFleet Journal 13 min read
Super Bowl LXI Ryder Cup 2027 Garcia vs Benn Money in the Bank LA28 Olympic Games

Five events are driving more ticket, hospitality and travel search volume right now than almost anything else on the global sporting calendar: Super Bowl LXI, the 2027 Ryder Cup, Ryan Garcia vs Conor Benn, WWE's Money in the Bank, and the LA28 Olympic Games. Each draws a different traveller — the corporate hospitality guest, the travelling athlete's family, the high-roller Vegas fight weekend crowd, the WWE superfan flying in for one night, the Olympic delegation moving through a car-free Games for seventeen days. What they share is a ground transport problem that ticket brokers, hospitality providers and even the leagues themselves rarely solve completely: getting from the airport to the hotel, and the hotel to the venue, without losing hours to surge pricing, road closures and a rideshare queue that stretches around the block.

This guide covers all five events in detail — dates, venues, airports, and exactly what VIP, player and fan transportation looks like for each one. It is built for the person who has already secured their tickets or their hospitality package and is now asking the question nobody else answers clearly: how do I actually move around on the day.

Why These Five Events Right Now

Super Bowl LXI, the Ryder Cup, Garcia vs Benn, Money in the Bank and LA28 sit at five different points on the calendar between now and 2028, but they share the same structural transport risk. Each concentrates tens of thousands of high-spending visitors into a single venue precinct on a single night or a single week, in cities whose everyday transport infrastructure was never built to absorb that volume. Rideshare surge pricing during major sporting events routinely runs two to four times the standard rate, and post-event pickup zones can mean waits of 60 to 90 minutes after the final whistle, the final putt, or the final bell. A pre-booked, flight-tracked, fixed-fare chauffeur removes every one of those variables before the trip even begins.

Super Bowl LXI VIP transportation covers corporate hospitality groups moving between suite-level entrances and hotel pickup points, executive sedans and SUVs for sponsors and their guests, and coordinated multi-vehicle fleets for companies entertaining clients across the full Super Bowl weekend — not just game day. Super Bowl LXI airport transfers from LAX, Burbank, Long Beach and Orange County are flight-tracked and confirmed before departure, which matters considerably more than usual during a week when every FBO and commercial terminal in Southern California is running above capacity with private aviation arrivals alongside commercial traffic.

The 2027 Ryder Cup — Adare Manor, County Limerick, September 13–19

DetailInformation
DatesSeptember 13–19, 2027 (practice rounds Mon–Thu, competition Fri–Sun)
VenueAdare Manor, County Limerick, Ireland
AirportsShannon International (SNN) — closest; Dublin (DUB) and Cork (ORK) as alternatives
Significance100th year of the event; first Ryder Cup return to Ireland since 2006

The Ryder Cup is unlike the other four events on this list in one important respect: it is not in a major metropolitan area. Adare Manor sits in rural County Limerick, and the transport reality of the week is built around shuttle hubs, not city taxi ranks. Official transport for ticket holders runs through the Limerick Colbert Station transport hub, with shuttle transfers to Adare Manor typically taking 30 to 40 minutes each way — a system that works for general admission but leaves a real gap for corporate hospitality guests, sponsors, and VIPs who need point-to-point private transport rather than a shared shuttle queue.

Ryder Cup 2027 VIP transportation from Shannon Airport — the closest international gateway, roughly a 30 to 45 minute drive from Adare Manor under normal conditions — is the single most valuable booking of the week for international visitors. Dublin Airport, while further out at roughly two hours by road, is the more common long-haul arrival point for visitors connecting from outside Europe, and a pre-booked Dublin-to-Limerick transfer removes the need to negotiate an unfamiliar Irish intercity route after a long flight. For corporate hospitality groups staying in Killarney or along the Shannon corridor, daily chauffeur transfers to Adare Manor — rather than the shared shuttle system — are the standard format for clients whose schedule cannot flex around a public transport timetable.

Unlike a single-venue Games, LA28 spreads competition across a genuinely vast footprint — Downtown LA, Inglewood, Pasadena, Carson, Long Beach, Santa Monica and the UCLA campus in Westwood are all active venue clusters simultaneously, each with its own access protocols and its own event-day traffic pattern. A hotel in Santa Monica might be a reasonable base for beach volleyball and sailing but a genuinely long transfer for a morning athletics session at SoFi Stadium. LA28 VIP transportation and delegation transport needs to be planned around which venue clusters a given day's schedule actually touches, not a single generic "Olympics transport" booking.

For delegations, corporate sponsors and family members of competing athletes, the standard visitors should plan around is 90 to 120 minutes of buffer before scheduled venue start times during peak Games days, given enhanced security screening and the sheer density of simultaneous arrivals. What VIP and Player Transportation Actually Requires

Across all five events, the transport requirement for VIPs, athletes, sponsors and hospitality guests is structurally different from a standard fan transfer, and the difference is not the vehicle badge.

Confirmed before departure, not arranged on arrival. Every one of these events sells out its professional ground transport capacity well before the event date. A visitor who waits until fight week, Ryder Cup week, or Games week to book a vehicle is competing against thousands of other attendees for a shrinking pool of available cars.

Flight tracking on every airport leg. LAX during Super Bowl week, Shannon during Ryder Cup week, LAS during a Mexican Independence Day fight weekend, MSY on a Money in the Bank Saturday, and every LA28 gateway airport across seventeen days of Games traffic will all see arrival volumes well above normal. A chauffeur tracking the actual flight, not a scheduled arrival time, is the only way to guarantee a positioned vehicle regardless of delay.

Confirmed post-event pickup positioning. The single most common failure point at every major event on this list is not the arrival — it is the exit. Tens of thousands of people leaving a venue simultaneously overwhelm standard pickup zones within minutes of the final whistle, putt, bell or match. A pre-briefed chauffeur positioned at a confirmed point away from the general crowd is the difference between a fifteen-minute departure and a ninety-minute one.

Multi-day, multi-venue account structure. None of these events are truly single-day bookings for the guests who matter most commercially — sponsors running hospitality programmes across a full Super Bowl weekend, families following an athlete through a full Ryder Cup week, delegations moving across LA28's seventeen-day, multi-venue footprint. A single account with a dedicated operations contact, rather than a new booking for every leg, is what actually holds up under that kind of schedule complexity.

Booking Ahead — Why It Matters More for These Five Events

Every one of these events shares a booking-window problem that most attendees discover too late. Professional ground transport operators with the vehicle standard and route knowledge these events demand book out weeks, sometimes months, before the event date — well before ticket holders start thinking seriously about their ground transport plan. For Super Bowl LXI and LA28, this window is now. For the Ryder Cup, Garcia vs Benn and Money in the Bank, it is measured in weeks rather than months given how close each date now sits on the calendar.
