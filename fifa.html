The Tournament

A World Cup Like No Other

The 2026 FIFA World Cup is the first to be jointly hosted by three nations and the first to feature an expanded field of 48 teams playing 104 matches over 39 days. The tournament opens on June 11 at the historic Estadio Azteca in Mexico City and culminates with the final on July 19 at MetLife Stadium in New York/New Jersey.

With matches spread across 11 cities in the United States, three in Mexico and two in Canada, getting between airports, hotels and stadiums on time — every time — is its own logistical challenge. That is exactly where NoirFleet comes in. Our chauffeur network operates in every host city, so wherever the tournament takes you, your car is already there.
